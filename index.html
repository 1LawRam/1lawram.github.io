How it actually goes

Print it. Break it. Print a better one.

Most of what I know came from the loop, not the lecture.

  1. Sketch the mechanism

    Paper first, then CAD. If I can't draw how the force travels through it, the model won't save me.

  2. Build the cheap version

    FDM parts and a breadboard. The goal isn't a finished thing — it's finding out which assumption was wrong.

  3. Instrument it

    Load cells, thermal arrays, a logic analyser. Opinions about why it failed are worth less than a trace.

  4. Write down what happened

    Every project here has a build log with the numbers in it, including the runs that didn't work.
