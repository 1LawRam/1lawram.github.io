How I work

I build the cheap version first.

Most of what I know came from building things and then fixing what went wrong.

  1. Sketch the mechanism

    I start on paper, then move to CAD. If I can’t draw how the force moves through a part, modelling it won’t help.

  2. Build the cheap version

    Printed parts and a breadboard. I’m not trying to finish anything yet, just to find out which assumption was wrong.

  3. Instrument it

    Load cells, thermal arrays, a logic analyser. Guessing why something failed takes longer than measuring it.

  4. Write down what happened

    Every project here has a build log with the real numbers in it, including the runs that didn’t work.
